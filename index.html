About the Event

What is AETHER WAVE?

AETHER WAVE is a premier hackathon organized by the IEEE Communications Society Student Branch Chapter (IEEE ComSoc SBC) at NSS College of Engineering (NSSCE), Palakkad, Kerala.

The name AETHER WAVE symbolizes the invisible medium through which signals, ideas, and innovations travel — from electromagnetic waves in hardware to data waves in software. It represents the convergence of electronics, communication technology, and digital innovation.

Participants will push boundaries, tackle real-world problems, and demonstrate their skills across hardware and software domains in an electrifying competitive environment.
